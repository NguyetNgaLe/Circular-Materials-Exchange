Detailed Description

This batch of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pellets is derived exclusively from clean, post-industrial streams. The rigorous melt-filtration process results in a highly consistent natural/white pellet suitable for direct replacement of virgin resins in non-food contact applications.

Ideal use cases include heavy-duty industrial packaging, drainage pipes, plastic lumber, and rugged automotive components. The low MFI makes it particularly well-suited for blow molding and extrusion processes requiring high melt strength.
